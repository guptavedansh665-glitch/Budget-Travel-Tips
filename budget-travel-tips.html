🌍 Travel Tips

🧳 1. Pack Smart and Light

Take only what you truly need. Choose clothes that can be mixed and matched. Roll clothes instead of folding — saves space! Keep essentials (charger, medicine, snacks) in your carry-on.

💳 2. Manage Your Money Wisely

Always carry a bit of local currency for small shops and transport. Notify your bank about international travel. Use travel cards or online wallets to avoid high exchange fees.

📱 3. Stay Connected

Get a local SIM card or data plan. Download offline maps. Keep copies of important documents in your cloud storage.

🍲 4. Try Local Food

Eat where the locals eat — it’s cheaper and tastier. Avoid raw foods and unfiltered water. Learn local phrases like “thank you.”

🏞️ 5. Respect Local Culture

Learn about customs and traditions. Always ask before taking photos. A smile and politeness go a long way.

🕐 6. Plan, But Stay Flexible

Have a general plan but allow spontaneity. Weather or local events may change your schedule — enjoy the surprises!
